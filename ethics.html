Restoration Ethics

Honoring the person behind every photograph.

Our Ethical Standards

Every restoration is guided by respect, honesty, and the belief that every photograph represents a real person, a real family, and a story worth preserving.

The Person Comes First

Restoration is never about replacing a person or creating someone new. Every decision begins with honoring the individual captured in the original photograph.

Preserving Identity

Every face carries unique expressions and characteristics. We restore photographs while carefully preserving those defining features so every person remains recognizable.

Respecting Family Memories

Many photographs represent loved ones who are no longer with us. Every image is handled with care, dignity, and deep respect for the memories it holds.

Custom Legacy Portraits

Legacy Portraits are artistic compositions created from individual photographs. They respectfully unite generations, celebrate family history, and preserve meaningful relationships while maintaining each person's identity.

Authenticity Matters

A restored photograph should feel like the person your family remembers. Our work focuses on enhancement, preservation, and respectful restoration rather than altering someone's appearance.

Creative Integrity

We clearly distinguish between authentic photograph restoration and artistic portrait creation. Every project is completed with honesty, transparency, and respect for the original memories being preserved.

Our Promise

Every photograph has value because every person has value. Whether restoring damaged family heirlooms or creating meaningful legacy portraits, we are committed to preserving identity, honoring memories, and treating every project with the care it deserves.

Every Face Has A Story

TruePortrait Revival exists to preserve those stories, protect family history, and help future generations remember the people who shaped their lives.
